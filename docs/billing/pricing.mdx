Fil One is priced at **$5.99 / TB / month**, with a **$5.99 / month minimum**. If you store less than 1 TB, you are billed the $5.99 minimum.

No egress fees, no per-request charges, no hidden costs.

This is the self-service **Pay as you go** plan. For volume pricing on 1, 3, or 5-year terms, the **Business plan** is arranged with the sales team. See [Billing](/app/billing#choosing-a-plan).

## How billing is calculated

Your bill is based on the average amount of data you store over the billing period:

- Stored bytes are measured as daily readings and averaged across the billing period. A metering job reports the running average **twice a day** (around 07:00 and 19:00 UTC).
- Storage is charged at a single flat rate of **$5.99 per TB per month**, applied to your average usage, with a **$5.99 / month minimum**. Any period that works out to less than $5.99 is billed at the $5.99 minimum. There is no separate "first TB" tier — it is one linear rate plus a floor.

Storage is measured precisely and in **decimal** units (1 TB = 1,000 GB), so you are billed for what you actually store rather than being rounded up to the next whole TB.

### Examples

Illustrative figures for a steady amount stored for the full month, at $5.99 per TB with the $5.99 monthly minimum applied:

| Average stored | Monthly bill |
|----------------|--------------|
| 500 GB | $5.99 (minimum applies) |
| 1 TB (1,000 GB) | ~$5.99 |
| 1.5 TB (1,500 GB) | ~$8.99 |
| 2 TB (2,000 GB) | ~$11.98 |

:::note
These figures are illustrative. Your invoice is the source of truth for exact amounts — open the **Billing** page in the dashboard to see your current usage and charges.
:::

If the amount you store changes during the month, it is averaged first. For example, storing 2 TB for the first 10 days and 0.5 TB for the remaining 20 days averages to 1 TB, billed at $5.99 for that month.

:::note If your account predates the current price
These are the prices for new customers. Organizations that subscribed before the move to $5.99 keep their earlier terms: $4.99 / TB / month with a $4.99 / month minimum, or, for accounts created before any monthly minimum was introduced, per-unit pricing with no minimum. The **Billing** page shows your own rate.
:::
