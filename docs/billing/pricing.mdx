Fil One is priced at **$4.99 / TB / month**, with a **$4.99 / month minimum**. If you store less than 1 TB, you are billed the $4.99 minimum.

No egress fees, no per-request charges, no hidden costs.

## How billing is calculated

Your bill is based on the average amount of data you store over the month:

- Stored bytes are sampled daily and averaged across the billing period.
- The first TB is billed at the flat $4.99 / month minimum. Storage above 1 TB is billed at $4.99 per additional TB, pro-rated to your exact usage and rounded to the nearest cent.

Usage is measured precisely, so you are billed for what you actually store above the first TB rather than being rounded up to the next whole TB.

### Examples

Assuming a steady amount stored for the full month:

| Average stored | Monthly bill | Why |
|----------------|--------------|-----|
| 500 GB | $4.99 | Below 1 TB, so the $4.99 minimum applies |
| 1,001 GB | $4.99 | 1 GB over 1 TB rounds to $0.00 |
| 1,002 GB | $5.00 | 2 GB over 1 TB is about $0.01 |
| 1,500 GB | $7.49 | $4.99 for the first TB plus $2.50 for the next 0.5 TB |

If the amount you store changes during the month, it is averaged first. For example, storing 2 TB for the first 10 days and 0.5 TB for the remaining 20 days averages to 1 TB, billed at $4.99 for that month.
