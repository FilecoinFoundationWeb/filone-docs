## Free trial

Every new account starts with a 30-day free trial. No credit card is required. The trial starts once you have verified your email address.

Each person gets **one trial**, on the first organization they own. Changing your email address does not grant a second one. Members invited to someone else's organization do not get a trial of their own. An organization you create after your first has **no trial**: it needs a payment method before you can use it, and it is billed separately, with its own $5.99 / month minimum.

| Included | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Storage | 1 TB (1,000 GB) |
| Egress bandwidth | 2 TB (2,000 GB) |
| API operations | Unlimited |
| Duration | 30 days from when the trial starts |

The trial gives you enough capacity to test real workloads, not just sample uploads. Almost everything works during the trial; the two exceptions are generating **shareable links** for objects and viewing **invoice history**, both of which unlock on a paid plan.

:::warning Trial caps are enforced, and the two caps behave differently
The storage and egress figures above are hard limits, not soft allowances, and reaching them has different consequences:

- **Storage reaching 1 TB** puts the account into a **write-locked** state — you can still read and download, but new uploads are rejected.
- **Egress reaching 2 TB** **disables the account** — the same state as an account whose grace period has ended, with no read or write access until you upgrade. There is no read-only grace period first.

Trial egress is a **single cumulative budget** across the whole 30 days, not a monthly amount that resets. Enforcement runs on a twice-daily usage job (around 07:00 and 19:00 UTC), so a cap may be exceeded for a few hours before access changes.
:::

## What happens when the trial ends

| Phase | Days | What you can do |
|-------|------|-----------------|
| **Active trial** | 0-30 | Full access. Upload, download, manage data. |
| **Grace period** | 31-37 | **Read-only.** Download your data and upgrade to a paid plan. No new uploads. |
| **Expired** | 38+ | Account disabled. No access to data, uploads, or API keys. An Owner can still reactivate from **Billing**. |

The trial ends at the end of day 30, but the read-only state is applied by a job that runs twice a day (08:00 and 20:00 UTC). Uploads over the S3 API can keep working for up to about 12 hours after the trial ends, until that job runs.

You will receive email reminders at:
- **Day 15** -- Halfway through your trial, with an upgrade link.
- **Day 25** -- 5 days left, with an upgrade link.
- **Day 31** -- Trial ended, grace period started.
- **Day 36** -- Grace period ends in 48 hours.
- **Day 38** -- Account disabled.

:::note
You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time during the trial or grace period, and an organization Owner can reactivate from **Billing** after the account is disabled too. Upgrading or reactivating restores full access immediately.
:::

:::info What "disabled" means
Disabling blocks all access to the account: your data is no longer reachable through the API or the dashboard, and access keys stop working. Fil One does not run an automatic purge at this point. To restore access, an organization Owner opens **Billing** and clicks **Reactivate**. To have your data removed, email [support@filone.com](mailto:support@filone.com).
:::

## Paid plan pricing

| Component | Price | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Storage | $5.99 / TB / month | Billed on average daily usage, minimum $5.99 / month. |
| Egress bandwidth | No charge | No egress fees. |
| API operations | Included | No per-request charges. |

Pricing is usage-based, measured as the average storage consumed across the billing period, with a **$5.99 / month minimum**. If your usage works out to less than $5.99 in a billing period, you are billed the $5.99 minimum. Organizations that subscribed before the move to $5.99 keep their earlier terms; see [Pricing](/billing/pricing). **Pay as you go** has no contract or term commitment. For volume pricing on 1, 3, or 5-year terms, the **Business plan** is arranged with the sales team. See [Billing](/app/billing#choosing-a-plan).

## Payment

Payments are processed through Stripe. An organization Owner adds a credit card from Billing in the dashboard, opened from **Billing** under **Organization** in the left sidebar. See [Billing](/app/billing).

- Invoices are generated monthly.
- Usage details are visible on the Billing page to Owners and Admins.
- Each invoice is charged automatically to the card on file.

## Payment failures

If a payment fails, your account enters **past due** status and Stripe automatically retries the charge. The **dashboard becomes read-only** from the moment the payment fails, and the Billing page shows **Your last payment failed** with an **Update payment** button.

If all retries are exhausted and the subscription is canceled by Stripe, your account transitions to the paid grace period (see below).

:::warning Check the Billing page, not your inbox
Don't rely on an email to tell you a payment has failed. If uploads stop working, open **Billing** in the dashboard: a **Your last payment failed** banner means the card needs updating, and an Owner can fix it with **Update payment**. See [Billing](/app/billing#your-plan).
:::

## Cancellation

An organization Owner can cancel the paid plan from the **Stripe billing portal**, which opens from **Billing** in the dashboard. Admins can see usage on Billing but can't open the portal. When cancellation takes effect depends on the option you choose there: if you cancel at the end of the current billing period, your plan stays **active and billable until that period ends**. The **30-day grace period** begins only once Stripe actually cancels the subscription — not the moment you click cancel:

| Phase | Duration | What you can do |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| **Grace period** | 30 days | **Read-only.** Download your data or reactivate your plan. No new uploads. |
| **Expired** | After grace period | Account disabled. No access to data, uploads, or API keys. |

Reactivating restores full access immediately, during the grace period or after the account is disabled: an organization Owner opens **Billing** and clicks **Reactivate**. Reactivating starts a new subscription, so your billing date moves to the day you reactivate. To have your data removed instead, email [support@filone.com](mailto:support@filone.com).
