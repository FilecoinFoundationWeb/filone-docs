title: Billing

Where billing lives depends on your organization. If your organization has an [Organization](/app/organization) page, billing is its **Billing** tab, and opening `/billing` takes you there. Otherwise, **Billing** sits at the bottom of the left sidebar, next to Settings.

Billing is visible to **Owners** and **Admins**. Every action on it (choosing or changing a plan, adding or updating a card, opening the Stripe portal) is for **Owners** only, so an Admin sees the same figures without the buttons. Members and Read only users see "Billing is managed by your organization's owners and admins" instead. See [Roles and permissions](/app/organization#roles-and-permissions).

This page covers the console specifically. For the trial lifecycle, the pricing model, and how usage is measured, see [Trial & Billing](/billing/trial), [Pricing](/billing/pricing), and [Usage](/billing/usage).

## Your plan

The **Plan** card names your plan and carries a status badge:

| Status | Badge | Plan name and detail line |
|--------|-------|---------------------------|
| Trial | **Trial** | **Free trial**, with **1 TB of storage included** and how many days until the trial ends |
| Paid | **Active** | **Pay as you go**, with your per-TB rate and the renewal date |
| Payment failed | **Payment failed** (amber) | Your plan name, rate, and renewal date, as for a paid plan |
| Grace period | **Grace period** | Your plan name and rate, and how many days until access ends |
| Canceled | **Canceled** | Your plan name and rate, and the date it was canceled |
| No plan | None | **No plan**, with **Choose a plan to start storing data** |

If your plan was set up with the sales team, the card shows the name on your agreement, and **Custom pricing** where there is no single per-TB rate to show.

The button in this card is **Upgrade** on the trial, **Manage plan** on a paid plan (including one whose payment failed), **Reactivate** once the subscription is canceled, and **Choose a plan** during the grace period or with no plan.

A banner above the cards flags a problem with the account:

| Banner | Button |
|--------|--------|
| **Your last payment failed** | **Update payment** |
| **This organization is read-only** (grace period) | **Choose a plan** |
| **This subscription is canceled** | **Reactivate** |

:::note A failed payment makes the dashboard read-only
When a payment fails, the dashboard becomes read-only. Check the banner and update your payment method to resume. See [Trial & Billing](/billing/trial).
:::

## Usage

The usage card is headed **Usage during your trial** on the trial and **Usage this billing period** otherwise, with the dates of the period and how many days are left.

**Storage** always appears. On the trial it is shown against the 1 TB cap with a progress bar; on a paid plan there is no cap, so you see the figure alone.

**Egress** also always appears. On the trial it is shown against the 2 TB cap with a progress bar. On a paid plan it is labelled **Free**, since there are no egress fees.

**Estimated total** appears on a paid plan with a per-TB rate. Where your monthly minimum is higher than your usage, the estimate shows the minimum, captioned **Your monthly minimum**. Plans with custom pricing show no estimate, and the card says the total for the period is in Stripe.

:::info How the estimate is calculated
The estimate applies your plan's per-TB rate to your most recent storage reading, with your monthly minimum as the floor. Your invoice is calculated from your average storage across the billing period, so treat the estimate as a guide to where you are heading rather than the final figure. Egress is not included, since it is free on paid plans.

Sizes here are decimal: 1 TB means 1,000 GB. See [Usage](/billing/usage).
:::

## Payment method

The **Payment method** card shows one of three things:

| What you see | Button |
|--------------|--------|
| **No card on file**, "Needed before your trial ends" | **Add card** |
| The card brand and last four digits, with the expiry date | **Update** |
| **Billed by invoice**, for a paid plan with no card on file | **Manage** |

**Update**, **Manage**, and **Manage plan** open the Stripe customer portal, where the card details are entered and stored. Fil One does not hold your card number.

:::note Cancelling
Cancellation is handled in the Stripe portal, reached through **Manage plan**.
:::

## Choosing a plan

**Add card**, **Upgrade**, **Choose a plan**, and **Reactivate** open **Choose your plan**, which offers two plans:

- **Pay as you go**, at **$4.99** per TB per month with a $4.99 monthly minimum, no egress fees, and no API request fees. **Upgrade now** takes you to the payment form.
- **Business plan**, with **Custom pricing**, purchased in 1, 3, or 5-year increments, with capacity assurance and deployment SLAs. **Contact sales team** opens a sales contact form.

The payment form takes your card number, expiry, and CVC, and an optional **Promo code** (3 to 40 letters, digits, or hyphens). **Start subscription** activates the plan.

If you are reactivating and a card is already on file, the **Pay as you go** button reads **Reactivate** and uses that card, with a **Use a different card** link if you would rather enter a new one.

## Invoices

The **Invoices** list shows the date, status, amount, and a **PDF** link for each invoice. Statuses are **Paid**, **Due**, **Void**, and **Unpaid**.

Three things to expect:

- The list does not appear during the trial or with no plan. Before your first invoice it reads **No invoices yet**.
- It shows your 12 most recent issued invoices, including any that are still unpaid. Drafts are not shown.
- **View all** opens the Stripe portal for your full billing history. It appears for Owners only.

## Need help?

The panel beside the cards offers **Billing questions**, which links to **Contact support** for questions about an invoice, a payment, or your plan. On the trial or with no plan, it also offers **Volume pricing**, where **Talk to sales** opens the sales contact form.

:::note If your account predates the monthly minimum
Accounts created before the minimum was introduced stay on per-unit pricing with no minimum. The plan card shows your per-TB rate, and your estimate is not raised to a minimum. The **Choose your plan** dialog shows the standard $4.99/month minimum wording, so take the plan card as the guide to your own terms. See [Pricing](/billing/pricing).
:::
