title: Support
description: How to reach the Fil One team from the console.
---

**Support** and **Documentation** are at the bottom of the left sidebar, above your name. **Support** opens the support page.

## The support page

The page is headed **Support**, with "We typically respond within 1 business day" underneath. It has two fields:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Category** | **Product issue**, **Billing issue**, **General inquiry**, or **Feature request** |
| **Message** | What you need help with |

You don't enter your name, organization, or email: the console sends them with your message from your account. Under the form, "We'll reply to" shows the address the answer goes to. An **Email** field appears only if your account has no email address. Click **Send message**. The form clears and confirms that the team will get back to you within 1 business day.

:::note Where the reply arrives
Your message creates a ticket for the support team, and they reply by email to your account's address. Look for the reply in your inbox, not in the console.
:::

Below the form, **Common questions** answers a few frequent questions, such as whether buckets can be made public. The **More help** panel beside it links to these docs and offers **Report a bug**.

## Other routes

- **Documentation** at the bottom of the left sidebar opens these docs in a new tab.
- The system status indicator links to the Fil One status page, which is the quickest way to check whether an issue is already known. On desktop it sits in the bar along the bottom of the content area; on a phone it is in the sidebar drawer.
- Account deletion is handled over email at [support@filone.com](mailto:support@filone.com), as noted in [Settings](/app/settings).
- For volume pricing, SLAs, or the Business plan, **Contact sales team** in the **Choose your plan** dialog on [Billing](/app/billing) reaches the sales team directly. On the trial or with no plan, **Talk to sales** in the **Need help?** panel beside the billing cards does the same.
