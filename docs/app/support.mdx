---
title: Support
description: How to reach the Fil One team from the console.
---

Support is reached from the **Help** menu at the bottom of the left sidebar, which offers **Documentation** and **Talk to an expert**. **Talk to an expert** opens the support form.

## The support form

The page is headed **Talk to an expert** and states that the team typically responds within one business day.

Every field is required:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **First name**, **Last name** | |
| **Company name** | |
| **Email** | The address the team replies to |
| **Category** | **Product Issue**, **Billing Issue**, **General Inquiry**, or **Feature Request** |
| **Message** | What you need help with |

The form starts empty rather than pre-filling from your account, so enter the email address you would like the reply sent to. Submitting clears the form and confirms that the team will get back to you within one business day.

:::note Where the reply arrives
Your message creates a ticket for the support team, and they reply by email, so keep an eye on the address you entered rather than looking for a thread in the console.
:::

## Other routes

- **Documentation** in the same **Help** menu opens these docs in a new tab.
- The sidebar links to the Fil One status page, which is the quickest way to check whether an issue is already known.
- Account deletion is handled over email at [support@fil.one](mailto:support@fil.one), as noted in [Settings](/app/settings).
- For volume pricing, SLAs, or the Business plan, **Contact sales** on the [Billing](/app/billing) page reaches the sales team directly.
