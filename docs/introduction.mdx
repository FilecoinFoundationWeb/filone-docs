## Why Fil One

**S3-compatible from day one.** Use the tools you already know — AWS CLI, boto3, any S3-compatible SDK. Point your existing code at your region's endpoint, turn on path-style addressing, and it works. See [S3 Compatibility](/reference/s3-compatibility) for the operations Fil One does not support.

**Backed by Filecoin.** Fil One stores your data on Filecoin, a distributed storage network where storage providers must continuously submit cryptographic proofs that they are still holding the data they committed to store, or face penalties.

**No egress fees on paid plans.** On a paid plan there are no data-transfer charges — download as much as you need. (The free trial has a 2 TB egress cap; see [Trial & Billing](/billing/trial).)

**Simple, flat pricing.** Pay as you go at $5.99 / TB / month, with a $5.99 / month minimum. No per-request charges, no hidden fees, no tiered pricing. For volume pricing on 1, 3, or 5-year terms, talk to sales about the Business plan.

**30-day free trial.** 1 TB of storage and 2 TB of bandwidth, no credit card required.
