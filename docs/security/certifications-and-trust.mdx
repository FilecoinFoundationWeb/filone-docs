This page summarizes the [Fil One Trust center](https://www.filone.com/trust). The Trust center is the authoritative source: if the two ever differ, go by the Trust center.

## What is certified today

Certification covers two scopes: the data centers that store your objects, and the network and software Fil One runs on top of them.

| Scope | Status | Certifications |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| Data centers | **Current** | All Fil One storage runs in data centers that are ISO/IEC 27001 certified, SOC 2 Type II attested, and PCI DSS certified. |
| Fil One (the network and software) | **In progress** | ISO/IEC 27001 certification and a SOC 2 Type II attestation are being pursued. |

Fil One itself is not yet SOC 2 or ISO/IEC 27001 certified as a company. The certifications above that are current apply to the data center facilities.

## Frameworks

| Framework | Status | What's available |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| HIPAA / HITECH | Supported | A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) on request. Sign a BAA with Fil One before storing any protected health information (PHI). |
| CJIS Security Policy | Supported | A CJIS Security Addendum. [Talk to sales](https://www.filone.com/contact-sales). |
| GDPR | Supported | A Data Processing Addendum (DPA) on request. You are the controller for personal data you store, and Fil One processes it on your instructions. |
| SOC 2 Type II | In progress | The report will be available under NDA after the audit. |
| ISO/IEC 27001 | In progress | The certificate will be available once Fil One is certified. |
| NIST SP 800-53 | In progress | A control mapping will be available once it's complete. |

## Where your data is stored

You choose each bucket's region when you create it: `eu-west-1` (Europe) or `us-east-1` (US). The region can't be changed later, so data written to a bucket stays where you put it. To keep data in the EU, create the bucket in `eu-west-1`. See [Buckets](/storage/buckets).

## Built-in controls

- **Encryption at rest:** AES-256 on every object, always on. You can also encrypt client-side before upload. See [Encryption](/security/encryption).
- **TLS on every request:** all traffic to the S3 endpoints runs over TLS.
- **Object Lock and versioning:** turned on per bucket, when you create it, for compliance retention. See [Object Lock](/storage/object-lock) and [Versioning](/storage/versioning).
- **Scoped access keys:** each key carries its own permissions and can be limited to specific buckets. See [API Keys](/security/api-keys).
- **Storage proofs:** Fil One runs on Filecoin, where storage providers continuously prove, with cryptographic proofs recorded on a public blockchain, that they still hold the data.

## Who holds the encryption keys

Server-side encryption keys are held by the regional storage operator that serves your bucket's region. If your policy requires you to hold the keys, encrypt client-side before upload. Fil One then stores only ciphertext. See [Client-side encryption](/security/encryption#client-side-encryption).
